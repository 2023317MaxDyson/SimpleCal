**Short answer:**  
No — **events**, **tasks**, and **appointments** are *three different object types* in digital calendars. An **event** does *not* classify as a task or an appointment, even though all three may appear in your calendar.   [Ellie](https://ellieplanner.com/productivity-guides/calendar-vs-to-do-list)

---

## 🧩 What each item actually represents

### **Event**
A **time‑bound commitment**.  
- Has a **start time**, **end time**, and often a **location** or participants.  
- Example: *Dentist appointment at 2:00 p.m.*, *Team meeting 10–11 a.m.*  
- Purpose: **When is this happening?**  
  [Ellie](https://ellieplanner.com/productivity-guides/calendar-vs-to-do-list)

### **Appointment**
A **specific type of event** that involves another person or a fixed external schedule.  
- All appointments are events, but not all events are appointments.  
- Example: *Doctor visit*, *Interview*, *Flight departure*.  
- Purpose: **You must be present at a specific time.**  
  [Ellie](https://ellieplanner.com/productivity-guides/calendar-vs-to-do-list)

### **Task**
An **action you need to complete**, not tied to a fixed time unless you choose to schedule it.  
- Can be done at any time unless you deliberately reserve time for it.  
- Example: *Draft pricing summary*, *Send invoice*, *Replace air filter*.  
- Purpose: **What action must be completed?**  
  [Ellie](https://ellieplanner.com/productivity-guides/calendar-vs-to-do-list)

---

## 🔍 Why people confuse them
Because apps like Google Calendar show tasks *on the calendar*, they can look like events — but they behave differently:

- **Events** block time and may involve others.  
- **Tasks** do not block time and remain until marked done.   [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/google-calendar-tasks-and-events-which-should-you-use/)  
- **Appointments** are simply events with external time constraints.

---

## 🧭 Quick classification rule
Use this to decide what something is:

1. **Does someone expect you at a specific time?**  
   → **Appointment (Event)**

2. **Does it reserve time on your calendar?**  
   → **Event**

3. **Is it an action you can do whenever?**  
   → **Task**
